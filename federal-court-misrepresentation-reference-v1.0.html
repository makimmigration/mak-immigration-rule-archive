Purpose

This reference helps practitioners distinguish recurring procedural-fairness and materiality questions in immigration misrepresentation cases: how clearly a concern must be disclosed, when undisclosed external material can matter, why the content of fairness remains contextual, and how the adequacy of a materiality analysis under A40(1)(a) depends on the reasons and record before the Court.

The table deliberately places materially different Federal Court reasoning side by side rather than presenting favourable cases in isolation.

Use boundary

This reference does not establish a universal full-disclosure rule, a universal second-PFL requirement, a rule that every misrepresentation case sits at the highest end of the fairness spectrum, or a rule that materiality can be presumed. Readers should verify current legislation, regulations, official guidance, complete reasons and current treatment before relying on a proposition in a live matter.
