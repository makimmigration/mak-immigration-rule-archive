MAK Immigration Data Observatory · Regulatory Archive v0.3.0

What was the immigration rule on a specific date?

A versioned archive of material Canadian immigration rule and policy changes, tied to official government sources. It preserves effective dates, superseded versions and transition rules instead of silently replacing old information.

Research/reference tool, not legal advice. The archive records what official sources state and when. Where an official page gives an announcement date but no separate legal effective date, the archive says so instead of inventing one.
