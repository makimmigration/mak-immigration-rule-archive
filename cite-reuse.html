Recommended archive citation

MAK Canadian Immigration Services, Immigration Data Observatory. Canadian Immigration Rule Change Archive, version 0.8.0, 2026-09-13. https://makimmigration.github.io/mak-immigration-rule-archive/

When citing an individual rule

Cite both the archive entry and the controlling official government source linked from that entry. The archive is the historical research layer; the underlying rule or policy remains attributable to the official authority.

MAK Canadian Immigration Services, Immigration Data Observatory. “{Event title / topic},” Canadian Immigration Rule Change Archive, v0.8.0. Controlling source: {official government authority and URL}. Verified {archive verification date}.

Machine-readable citation metadata

The repository publishes CITATION.cff for software and research tooling. The canonical structured dataset is events.json; a flat analyst export is available as events.csv.
