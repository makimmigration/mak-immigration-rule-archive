What the released table measures

The released material covers January 1, 2019 through March 31, 2023 and was extracted from COGNOS (MBR) on April 26, 2023. IRCC's source footnote states that one application may be refused for more than one refusal ground. The figures are therefore refusal-ground instances, not unique refused applications.

Legal-code controls

Direct first-party cross-references were verified for A11(1), A16(1), A40(1)(a), A40(1), A41(a), A42(1)(b), R89 and legacy MI SUD-2(5). Two source-system labels remain intentionally unresolved:

  • R98(1): literal former IRPR R98(1) concerned the former entrepreneur class and was repealed before this dataset period. This release contains no first-party COGNOS codebook proving that the label should be rewritten to R98.01(1) or R98.10(1).
  • A89: literal IRPA A89 concerns fee regulations and does not establish what this COGNOS label means in the refusal table.

The canonical reference preserves the full source map and primary legal links.

Current-program boundary

IRCC currently states that the Start-Up Visa Program was paused on June 30, 2026, that new permanent-residence applications are not being accepted, and that applications accepted before that date continue to be processed. This historical dataset should therefore be used for research into past decision patterns and pending inventory, not as a current intake guide.

Interpretation limits

  • 223 is not a proven count of 223 unique refused applications.
  • The table is a top-10 refusal-ground table, not every refusal ground.
  • The release does not cover the full broader 2018–2025 period originally requested.
  • 2023 is partial-year data through March 31.
  • Frequency is not an approval or refusal probability.
  • Unresolved labels must not be given invented modern legal meanings.
